How it works

  1. Take a clear photo

    Get close, use good daylight, and keep the problem area in the middle of the photo.

  2. Upload it

    We check the photo is sharp and bright enough, then the AI picks the right check for it.

  3. Read your result

    See what it looks most like, safe things to try at home, and skin doctors near you.

After your check, you get

  • A clear answer in plain wordsWhat your photo looks most like, and how close the match is.
  • Safe home careSimple tips for that condition and common medicines to ask a pharmacist about.
  • Skin doctors near youClinics in your city with buttons to call, get directions or ask for an appointment.
  • An honest "not sure"If the photo is unclear, we say so instead of guessing.
